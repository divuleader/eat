1Chia Pudding (Your Recipe, Refined)
~310 cal / serving~12g protein3 servings
Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond or oat milk
  • 2 tsp monk fruit sweetener
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Topping per serving: ½ cup mixed berries + 1 tbsp almond butter
Steps
  1. Whisk chia seeds into milk with sweetener and vanilla.
  2. Refrigerate overnight or 4+ hours, stirring once after 30 min.
  3. Portion into 3 jars. Top with fruit and almond butter to serve.

No cook. One of the highest-protein breakfasts in this guide — great for class days when you need fuel that lasts.

10Warm Egg Drop Soup (Savory Breakfast)
~280 cal / serving~20g protein2 servings
Ingredients

  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • ½ cup chopped scallions

1Seasoned Shrimp Stir-Fry (Your Recipe)
~380 cal / serving~38g protein2 servings
Ingredients
  • ½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • ½ bell pepper, ¼ onion, 1 carrot — all chopped
  • 1 tsp avocado oil
  • ½ tsp Maggi Masala seasoning
  • ½ tsp garlic paste, ½ tsp ginger paste
  • Salt, pepper, squeeze of lemon
Steps
  1. Heat oil in a skillet. Sauté onion + garlic + ginger paste 2 minutes.
  2. Add carrot and bell pepper, cook 3 minutes until slightly softened.
  3. Add shrimp and Maggi Masala. Cook until shrimp turns pink, about 3–4 minutes.
  4. Finish with a squeeze of lemon. Serve as is or over cauliflower rice.

3Chicken Tikka (No Rice)
~410 cal / serving~42g protein2 servings
Ingredients
  • 12 oz boneless skinless chicken thigh, cubed
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt (for marinade)
  • 1 tsp each: garam masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, chili powder
  • 2 garlic cloves + 1 tsp ginger (for marinade)
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes, ¼ cup heavy cream or coconut cream
  • 1 tsp avocado oil
Steps
  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt + spices + garlic + ginger for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Grill or broil chicken until cooked through with a slight char.
  3. In the same pan, sauté tomatoes in oil, add remaining spices, then add cream. Simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Toss grilled chicken into the sauce. Serve with cucumber slices or cauliflower rice.

9Zucchini Noodle Shrimp Scampi
~380 cal / serving~37g protein2 servings
Ingredients
  • ½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 medium zucchini, spiralized or peeled into ribbons
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced thin
  • 1 tbsp butter + 1 tsp avocado oil
  • ¼ cup dry white wine or chicken broth
  • Lemon juice, red pepper flakes, fresh parsley, salt and pepper
Steps
  1. Sauté garlic in butter and oil for 1 minute over medium heat.
  2. Add shrimp and cook until pink, about 3 minutes. Remove shrimp.
  3. Deglaze pan with wine or broth. Add lemon juice and red pepper flakes.
  4. Add zucchini noodles for 1–2 minutes only (they release water fast — don't overcook).
  5. Return shrimp to pan. Toss, plate, and top with parsley.
10Baked Chicken Thighs with Roasted Cauliflower
~470 cal / serving~44g protein2 servings
Ingredients
  • 2 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (or 3 boneless skinless)
  • 1 tsp each: smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, dried oregano, salt
  • 1 tsp avocado oil
  • 1 small head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • Side sauce: 2 tbsp Greek yogurt + lemon + garlic + za'atar (or tahini)
Steps
  1. Rub chicken with oil and the full spice blend.
  2. Roast at 425°F for 35 minutes total.
  3. After 10 minutes, add cauliflower to the same pan with oil and salt.
  4. Rest 5 minutes before serving. Serve with yogurt dipping sauce.

General rules: ask for sauces on the side, skip the starch (rice, bread, tortillas, noodles unless they're vegetable-based), and always anchor the meal around a grilled or baked protein.
🇮🇳 Indian / Pakistani
Tandoori chicken or seekh kebabs — protein-heavy, minimal sauce
Chicken tikka dry (ask for sauce on the side, or skip it)
Dal (lentil soup) — good fiber and protein, watch portion size
Raita as a side instead of naan
Boti kabab, reshmi kabab, or any grilled skewer dish
⚠️ Avoid: naan, rice, samosas, korma and butter chicken (very high cream), biryani
🌮 Mexican / Tex-Mex
Protein bowl — chicken, shrimp, or steak over lettuce + pico + guac, no rice or beans
Fajitas — eat the protein and peppers/onions as a bowl, skip tortillas
Ceviche — naturally low carb, high protein, very light
Guacamole with veggie dippers instead of chips (ask for cucumber or jicama)
⚠️ Avoid: tortillas, chips (unlimited chips = easy 400+ extra calories), rice, tamales, churros
🇯🇵 Japanese
Sashimi (salmon, tuna, yellowtail) — pure protein, zero rice
Edamame + miso soup as starters (filling, low cal)
Yakitori — grilled chicken skewers, ask for tare sauce on the side
Chirashi bowl — request less rice or a side salad substitution
Agedashi tofu (lightly fried, lighter than most options)
⚠️ Avoid: tempura, fried gyoza, ramen (high carb + sodium), sushi rolls (rice-heavy)
🇰🇷 Korean
Mul-naengmyeon — cold broth noodle soup, very light overall
Bulgogi lettuce wraps — skip the rice, wrap in lettuce
Doenjang jjigae (soybean paste soup with tofu and veggies)
Sundubu jjigae (soft tofu stew, protein-rich)
Banchan (small side dishes) — most are vegetable-based and low cal
⚠️ Avoid: Korean fried chicken (unless a planned treat), bibimbap with rice, japchae (glass noodles)
🍔 American / Fast Casual
Chipotle bowl: chicken or steak + fajita veggies + salsa + guac + lettuce — NO rice, NO beans
Grilled chicken sandwich — ditch the bun, eat the protein and toppings as a plate
Brunch omelets or scrambles — ask for extra veggies in place of toast or home fries
Side salad with grilled protein — dressing on the side (use half)
⚠️ Avoid: fries, burger buns, creamy dressings in large amounts, milkshakes, pasta bowls
🧆 Mediterranean / Middle Eastern
Grilled kebabs (lamb kofta, chicken, shish tawook) with a fattoush or tabbouleh salad
Hummus with raw veggies (not pita) as a starter
Grilled fish with charmoula or lemon-herb dressing
Shakshuka (eggs poached in spiced tomato sauce) — very filling, often available at brunch
Labneh with cucumber and olives as a side
⚠️ Avoid: pita, lavash, rice pilaf, baklava, knafeh
